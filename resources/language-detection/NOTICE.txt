Changes by the Typlo contributors: NFC/lowercase normalization; normalization
of apostrophes/hyphens; rejection of numbers, malformed tokens and incompatible
scripts (Cyrillic orthography for ru/uk, Latin letters including loans for the
other six languages); combination of normalized duplicates; combination of
all eight lists with separate frequency weights; logarithmic quantization;
storage in an indexed SQLite database. No words have been invented or translated.
This is a frequency vocabulary, not an authoritative spelling dictionary.
