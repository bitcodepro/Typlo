Typlo Rust distributes the EN, RU and UK Hunspell dictionary files from
Typlo v0.5.14. The corresponding editable dictionary sources are the .aff
and .dic files beside this notice. See the dictionary license files and
the pinned LibreOffice dictionaries commit in manifest.json.

Typlo Rust uses Spellbook 0.4.2 as its spell-checking engine. The
WeCantSpell engine mentioned in Typlo's original NOTICE and manifest is
not included in Typlo Rust. Spellbook is licensed under MPL 2.0.
The corresponding source is available at
https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/spellbook/0.4.2/download
and its license appears in LICENSE-Spellbook.txt.
